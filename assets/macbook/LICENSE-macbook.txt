macbook.glb is based on "MacBook Pro 16" 2021"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/macbook-pro-16-2021-8ac16bda501c4bcc8ba93ac3eb63a8e1)
by rtql8d (https://sketchfab.com/rtql8d), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for reelscript:
- The keyboard painted on the casing texture is replaced by a plain dark well
  (reelscript draws its own key caps over it).
- The lid's back is a plain aluminium colour (its texture, with a logo, removed).
- The screen's picture is removed (reelscript shows the demo there).
- Packed as one binary glTF (GLB) and unused data pruned.
